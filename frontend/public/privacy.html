Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Prism ("we," "us") collects when you use prisminv.com (the "Service"), how we use it, and who we share it with. By using the Service you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: your email address, managed through our authentication provider (Supabase). If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and basic profile from Google.
  • Payment data: processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation of a successful payment and a customer reference, but we never receive or store your full card number — Stripe handles that directly.
  • Usage data: the tickers you analyze and the Deep Research reports generated for your account, stored so you can revisit them; and optional thumbs up/down feedback you submit.
  • Technical & analytics data: aggregate usage and basic device/page analytics (via Vercel Analytics), and request metadata such as IP address used for rate-limiting and abuse prevention.
  • Local storage: we store small items in your browser (e.g. a cached account summary and your last viewed ticker) to keep the app responsive. These are not used for cross-site tracking.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the Service: authenticate you, run analyses, save your research history, and apply your credits/subscription.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To communicate with you about your account, purchases (including receipts), and important changes.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. You can also request account deletion directly.

6. Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable technical measures (encrypted connections, scoped access keys, hosted authentication) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
